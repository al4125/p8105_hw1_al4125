In the penguins dataset, it gives us information on the type of species,
island, bill length, bill depth, flipper length, body mass, sex, and
year. The three types of species in the dataset are Adelie, Gentoo, and
Chinstrap. The types of islands are Torgersen, Biscoe, and Dream, and
the dataset includes both male and female penguins. Some of the values
in the dataset are NA, showing that they are missing.

There are 344 rows and 8 columns.

The mean of flipper length is 200.92 mm.
